Welcome to the Jari playground

This page pretends the Jari extension is installed. Keep your hands on the home row: jari lets you scroll with the keyboard, jump to links with hints, search page text with find, and select text with visual mode. Every keyboard shortcut below works right now. See the full key guide for history (H/L) and page keys (gu/gU).

Start with scrolling: press j and k a few times, then try 3j, d for half a page, gg for the top and G for the bottom. The keyboard stays in charge the whole time.

Links are just a hint away

Press f and a yellow label appears on every link. Type the label to follow it — f opens here, F opens a new tab, and gf opens a background tab. Try it: jari repo, releases, issues, privacy notes.

More targets for longer labels: readme, changelog, security, example.com, example.org. Buttons work too: .

Find in page

Press /, type keyboard or jari, then Enter. Step through matches with n and N (counts work: 2n). Esc hides the highlights, n brings them back, and Enter on a link match follows it. Search is smart-case: lowercase matches anything, uppercase is exact.

The keyboard rewards repetition. A keyboard-first flow means less reaching for the mouse, and jari keeps the keyboard close: scroll, hint, find, yank, repeat. Search this paragraph for keyboard — it appears several times on purpose so n has somewhere to go.

Visual mode and help

Press v to drop a caret into this article, move with h/j/k/l, and press y to yank the selection to your clipboard. Esc exits. Press ? any time for the full demo cheatsheet.

More room to scroll

This section exists so scrolling has somewhere to go. Press j ten times and watch the page glide, then d twice for two half-page jumps. The keyboard never leaves your fingers while the jari playground slides underneath it.

Counts multiply everything: 5j drops five steps, 5k climbs back up, 2d falls a full page. Try G to slam to the bottom, then gg to snap back to the top. Feel the distance — that is the point of this extra space.

Scrolling deep dive

Half pages are the workhorse of keyboard scrolling. d moves down half a viewport, u moves up half a viewport, keeping your eyes near the text you were reading. Walk down this whole article with d d d and back up with u u u.

The keyboard rewards small motions compounded. A single j is a nudge; a 3j is a stride; a d is a leap. Mix them: d, 3j, j, u. The jari way is always the keyboard, never the scrollbar.
