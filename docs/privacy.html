No collection, period

Jari does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything it reads stays on your device. Full text lives in PRIVACY.md; report concerns via a private security advisory.

What the extension touches, and why

Page content (<all_urls>, all frames) renders hints, scrolling, find, and visual mode — processed in the page and discarded. Tabs, tab groups (Chrome only), and sessions power J/K switching, x close/reopen, gt manager, and gr restoring recently closed tabs and windows. History and bookmarks feed the t omnibox suggestions. The page history tree (gh) keeps each visited page's URL and title in a device-local IndexedDB snapshot, erased when the browser starts a new session. Clipboard read opens a URL from your clipboard (gp/gP); clipboard write copies URLs and titles (yy, Y, yf). Screenshots save the viewport to your downloads on your keypress. All of it goes nowhere.

Where settings live

Keybindings and options persist in your browser's synced storage — never sent to us. Find history (/ queries recalled with Up/Down) stays in local storage on this device only, never synced. See settings for what can be configured.

Network use

Jari makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity it can trigger is navigation or search you explicitly request — same as typing in the address bar.
