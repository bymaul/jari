Keybindings

Click a key field (or focus it and press Enter), then press the key combination to bind it. Esc cancels, Backspace clears. Digits 0-9 are reserved for the repeat count. Prefix keys (g, ;, y) bind as pairs — press the prefix, then the next key. Bindings save immediately.

Disabled sites

Jari is turned off on these sites. Press on a page to toggle it there, or add a host here.

    To open these settings anytime, press on any page (or find Jari under chrome://extensions).
