Jari

Keyboard-driven navigation settings

Behavior

px

Distance covered by one j/k or h/l press.

Animate scrolling instead of jumping straight to the position.

ms

How long a half-typed g prefix or count waits before clearing.

Used for highlights: link hints, headings, the primary button and the showcmd edge.

Keybindings

Click a key field, then press the key (or key combination) to bind it. Backspace clears a binding. Digits 0-9 are reserved for the repeat count and cannot be bound. Fixed prefixes such as gg, gt and ;s are shown in green and can only be cleared.

Disabled sites

Jari is turned off on these sites. Press ctrl+alt+v on a page to toggle it there.

    To open these settings anytime, press ; s on any page (or find Jari under chrome://extensions).
